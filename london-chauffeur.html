FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas of London does Ridealux cover?

Ridealux covers all of London including central areas, outer boroughs, all five airports, and surrounding counties. Nationwide UK travel is also available on request.

How do I book a chauffeur in London?

Visit ridealux.com to make an instant online booking. Select your service type, enter journey details, choose your vehicle, and receive immediate confirmation with your chauffeur details.

Does Ridealux operate 24 hours?

Yes. Ridealux is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year — including early morning, late night, and public holiday travel.

Are Ridealux prices fixed?

Yes. All journeys are quoted with fixed, transparent pricing. There are no surge charges, no hidden fees, and your price never changes once confirmed.

Can Ridealux handle large group travel in London?

Absolutely. Ridealux provides multi-vehicle coordination for groups of all sizes, including corporate teams, delegations, and event guests requiring several vehicles simultaneously.
