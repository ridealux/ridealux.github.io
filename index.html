FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Ridealux cover?

Ridealux operates across all of London and covers all five major London airports including Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, and London City Airport. We also provide nationwide UK travel on request, including journeys to other major UK cities.

Does Ridealux offer fixed pricing?

Yes. All Ridealux journeys are priced transparently with no hidden charges or surge pricing. Your quote is confirmed at the time of booking and will not change regardless of traffic or journey duration.

Can I book a chauffeur for a full day?

Absolutely. Ridealux offers flexible full-day and half-day private chauffeur hire for clients who require dedicated transportation for extended periods. Your chauffeur remains at your disposal for the entire duration.

How are flight delays handled?

Ridealux monitors all flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, your chauffeur automatically adjusts their arrival time — so you are never waiting and never paying for unnecessary extra time at the airport.

Is Ridealux available 24 hours?

Yes. Ridealux operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Early morning departures, late night arrivals, overnight bookings, and public holiday travel are all fully accommodated.

Can Ridealux handle corporate account bookings?

Yes. Ridealux works with corporate clients and businesses to establish account-based booking arrangements with consolidated monthly invoicing, dedicated account management, and priority service.
